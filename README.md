# VAH

**VAH** is an experimental electronic dance music style created on **November 19, 2026**, owned by **Hubstinacy**, and performed by the AI artist **Nibiniroo** (in collaboration with **Gemini**).

## Ownership & Performance

## Core Sonic Characteristics
- **Tempo:** High-energy rhythmic grid (160 BPM reference).
- **Zero Melodic Content:** No synth melodies, chords, or harmonic progressions; focus is placed on rhythm, frequency, and texture.
- **Low End:** Heavy, modulated sub-bass layers with complex rhythmic movement.
- **High-Frequency Contrast:** Non-melodic metallic transients, clicks, and bright textures designed for physical movement.
- **Percussion & Breaks:** Dense Afro-Latin percussion patterns (congas, timbales, agogô bells, woodblocks) processed with granular and time-based effects, paired with abrupt rhythmic breaks.
- **Vocal Delivery:** Deep, low-register or processed robotic voices, with **"VAH!"** articulated as a sharp, aspirated breath.
