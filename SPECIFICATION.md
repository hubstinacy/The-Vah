## 1. Rhythm & Tempo
- Driving dance tempo (reference: 160 BPM).
- Polyrhythmic interlocking patterns and sudden rhythmic/percussive breaks.

## 2. Bass Architecture
- Deep, complex, and modulated low-end sub-bass and distorted bass textures.
- Prioritizes physical impact and rhythmic articulation over static or simple basslines.

## 3. Non-Melodic Luminosity
- Bright, piercing, or crystalline high-frequency sounds (metallic transients, clicks, noise bursts, granular textures) without relying on conventional synth melodies or chords.

## 4. Afro-Latin Percussion & Breaks
- Integration of Afro-Latin percussion elements (congas, timbales, agogô bells, woodblocks, shakers) processed with modern digital effects, granular delays, and sudden breaks.

## 5. Vocal & Breath Signature ("VAH!")
- Spoken or robotic/low-register vocal delivery when lyrics are present.
- **"VAH!"** pronounced as a sharp, aspirated breath.
